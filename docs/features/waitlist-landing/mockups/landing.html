FAQ

Questions

When can I use it?

We open access in waves. People higher on the list get in first, and each friend you invite moves you up.

How much will it cost?

Pricing comes with the launch. The waitlist hears first.

Do I need to be a developer?

No. If you use ChatGPT, Claude or another AI, you connect it by pasting one line.

Does it message other people?

No. zapbip talks only to you, on your own phone, through the official WhatsApp Cloud API.

Can several agents share it?

Yes. All your agents share one number, and each reply goes back to the agent that asked.

How do invites work?

After you join, you get a personal link. Each friend who joins with it moves you up the list. There is a limit per person, shown after you join.

What do you do with my email?

We use it only to invite you when access opens. No IP address, no tracking cookies. To be removed, write to support@zapbip.com.
