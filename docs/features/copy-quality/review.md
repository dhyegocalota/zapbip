The legal sentence "what you decide from an agent message, such as a button tap" now names a reply in all eight languages; wording only.

## Kept on purpose

Instructions that tell the person to press a real button on a real screen: "tap Open" in the templates, "Tippe auf einen Button", "ボタンをタップ", "버튼을 탭", "点按使用 WhatsApp 登录". The pt, es and fr docs say "choose" for the same sign-in step. Keyboard keys ("pressione Ctrl+C") are untouched. The pt-BR site line "Você responde com um toque" is the owner's own example of the right form and has a test.

## Unsure

- Owner is named differently by surface in several languages (site story voice "dueño", "Besitzer", "主人"; docs "propietario", "Owner", "所有者"). Left as is: the story voice and the reference voice differ on purpose.
- "IA" in docs against "agente" in replies is recorded in the glossary as an existing choice.
- The Meta templates in the six newer languages are still the English ones until approved; their wording is for the owner to confirm at submission.
