## What works

- Your AI sends you text, photos, videos, audio, documents, locations, contacts and reactions on WhatsApp.
- It asks you with buttons or lists and waits for your tap.
- Write on WhatsApp and Codex answers in about 6 seconds, even when the session is idle.
- Codex waits up to 15 minutes for a single answer and asks again, so there is no limit overall.

## Set up from zero

<Steps>
  <Step title="Message the official zapbip number">
    Open WhatsApp and send `hi` to the official zapbip number, then `new agent codex`. [How to start](/get-started/how-to-start) shows every message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the prompt into Codex">
    zapbip answers with one line made for you. Paste it into Codex:

    ```text
    Connect yourself to my WhatsApp with zapbip: read https://zapbip.com/i/zb_inst_K7P2QXm9aTfLw3VbN8cYhE and follow the instructions.
    ```

    Codex reads the page and follows the steps below, in order. Before it changes a file or a setting, it tells you which one and waits for your yes. The link in that page is secret, so keep it out of chats you share.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add zapbip to Codex">
    After your yes, it replaces the `[mcp_servers.zapbip]` table in `~/.codex/config.toml` with `url = "<connectionLink>"`, `tool_timeout_sec = 900` and `default_tools_approval_mode = "approve"`. Approval mode lets zapbip tools run without asking you each time, and Codex tells you so.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connector">
    It runs `ZAPBIP_CONNECTION_URL=<connectionLink> npx zapbip@0.1.0 connect --client codex` in the background. It keeps listening and resumes your session with `codex exec resume`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    In a fresh process it runs `codex exec -c 'mcp_servers.zapbip.default_tools_approval_mode="approve"' "Use zapbip to send ✅ installed to my WhatsApp"`. A `send_message` with ✅ installed reaches your WhatsApp. If the command fails, it stops and shows the last 20 lines. Then restart Codex.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How your AI listens

- The connector stays open and resumes your Codex session with `codex exec resume` each time you write, in about 6 seconds.
- If Codex already has the session open in its app, the resume waits until the session is free.

There is no time limit. While a question is open, your AI asks again until you answer, for as long as it takes.
