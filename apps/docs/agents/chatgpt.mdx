## What works

- Your AI sends you text, photos, videos, audio, documents, locations, contacts and reactions on WhatsApp.
- It asks you with buttons or lists and waits for your tap.
- ChatGPT reads your WhatsApp messages once an hour, the shortest interval the app allows.
- ChatGPT waits about 5 minutes for a single answer and asks again, so there is no limit overall.

## Set up from zero

<Steps>
  <Step title="Message the official zapbip number">
    Open WhatsApp and send `hi` to the official zapbip number, then `new agent chatgpt`. [How to start](/get-started/how-to-start) shows every message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the prompt into ChatGPT">
    zapbip answers with one line made for you. Paste it into ChatGPT:

    ```text
    Connect yourself to my WhatsApp with zapbip: read https://zapbip.com/i/K7P2QX and follow the instructions.
    ```

    ChatGPT reads the page and follows the steps below, in order. Before it changes a file or a setting, it tells you which one and waits for your yes. The link in that page is secret, so keep it out of chats you share.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app">
    ChatGPT asks you to turn on developer mode and add an app by URL with `<connectionLink>`, choosing "No authentication". You do this one yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the hourly task">
    It asks you to create an hourly "Run on this computer" task that calls `check_inbox`, in one dedicated chat, and to choose "Always allow" once for each tool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell zapbip it polls">
    It calls `set_wake({mode: "scheduled", interval_minutes: 60})`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    In the chat it calls `send_message` with ✅ installed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How your AI listens

- The hourly task runs on your computer and posts into the same chat. Each run reads what you wrote since the last one, so a message can wait up to an hour.
- ChatGPT allows no shorter interval for scheduled tasks.

There is no time limit. While a question is open, your AI asks again until you answer, for as long as it takes.
