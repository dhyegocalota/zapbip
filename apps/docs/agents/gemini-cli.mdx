## What works

- Your AI sends you text, photos, videos, audio, documents, locations, contacts and reactions on WhatsApp.
- It asks you with buttons or lists and waits for your tap.
- Write on WhatsApp and Gemini CLI hears you while its session is open.
- It waits for your answer as long as it takes.

## Set up from zero

<Steps>
  <Step title="Message the official zapbip number">
    Open WhatsApp and send `hi` to the official zapbip number, then `new agent gemini-cli`. [How to start](/get-started/how-to-start) shows every message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the prompt into Gemini CLI">
    zapbip answers with one line made for you. Paste it into Gemini CLI:

    ```text
    Connect yourself to my WhatsApp with zapbip: read https://mcp.zapbip.com/i/zb_inst_K7P2QXm9aTfLw3VbN8cYhE and follow the instructions.
    ```

    Gemini CLI reads the page and follows the steps below, in order. Before it changes a file or a setting, it tells you which one and waits for your yes. The link in that page is secret, so keep it out of chats you share.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add zapbip to Gemini CLI">
    After your yes, it runs `gemini mcp add --transport http --scope user zapbip <connectionLink>`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start listening">
    It runs `ZAPBIP_CONNECTION_URL=<connectionLink> npx zapbip@0.1.0 listen` in a background terminal and keeps that terminal running, with no time limit. Whenever a new line appears there, it calls `check_inbox` and answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    In a fresh process it runs `gemini -p "Use zapbip to send ✅ installed to my WhatsApp"`. A `send_message` with ✅ installed reaches your WhatsApp. If the command fails, it stops and shows the last 20 lines. Then restart Gemini CLI.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How your AI listens

- Gemini CLI runs `npx zapbip@0.1.0 listen` in a background terminal and keeps it running. When you write on WhatsApp, the new line wakes the session.
- Gemini CLI has no scheduled option, so keep the terminal running. It has no time limit.

There is no time limit. While a question is open, your AI asks again until you answer, for as long as it takes.
