## What works

- Your AI sends you text, photos, videos, audio, documents, locations, contacts and reactions on WhatsApp.
- It asks you with buttons or lists and waits for your tap.
- Write on WhatsApp and Cursor hears you while its chat is open.
- It waits for your answer as long as it takes.

## Set up from zero

<Steps>
  <Step title="Message the official zapbip number">
    Open WhatsApp and send `hi` to the official zapbip number, then `new agent cursor`. [How to start](/get-started/how-to-start) shows every message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the prompt into Cursor">
    zapbip answers with one line made for you. Paste it into Cursor:

    ```text
    Connect yourself to my WhatsApp with zapbip: read https://zapbip.com/i/zb_inst_K7P2QXm9aTfLw3VbN8cYhE and follow the instructions.
    ```

    Cursor reads the page and follows the steps below, in order. Before it changes a file or a setting, it tells you which one and waits for your yes. The link in that page is secret, so keep it out of chats you share.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add zapbip to Cursor">
    It asks you first and waits for your yes. Then it adds an MCP server named `zapbip` over HTTP at `<connectionLink>` in Cursor's MCP settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start listening">
    It runs `ZAPBIP_CONNECTION_URL=<connectionLink> npx zapbip@<version> listen` in a background terminal and keeps that terminal running, with no time limit. Whenever a new line appears there, it calls `check_inbox` and answers. `<version>` is the exact version your AI's instructions name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    In the chat it calls `send_message` with ✅ installed. Then restart the Cursor chat to use the zapbip tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How your AI listens

- Cursor runs `npx zapbip@<version> listen` in a background terminal and keeps it running. When you write on WhatsApp, the new line wakes the chat.
- Cursor has no scheduled option, so keep the terminal running. It has no time limit.

There is no time limit. While a question is open, your AI asks again until you answer, for as long as it takes.
