## What works

- Your AI sends you text, photos, videos, audio, documents, locations, contacts and reactions on WhatsApp.
- It asks you with buttons or lists and waits for your tap.
- Grok on the web sends you messages and asks questions while its chat is open.
- It waits 1 minute for a single answer and asks again, so there is no limit overall.
- For writing first, a Grok Bot of yours stands in. Without one, Grok sees your messages when you open its chat.

## Set up from zero

<Steps>
  <Step title="Message the official zapbip number">
    Open WhatsApp and send `hi` to the official zapbip number, then `new agent grok-web`. [How to start](/get-started/how-to-start) shows every message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the prompt into Grok on the web">
    zapbip answers with one line made for you. Paste it into Grok on the web:

    ```text
    Connect yourself to my WhatsApp with zapbip: read https://zapbip.com/i/K7P2QX and follow the instructions.
    ```

    Grok on the web reads the page and follows the steps below, in order. Before it changes a file or a setting, it tells you which one and waits for your yes. The link in that page is secret, so keep it out of chats you share.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the connector">
    Grok asks you to paste `<connectionLink>` as a connector in its settings. You do this one yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick who answers when you write first">
    If you have a Grok Bot connected to zapbip, Grok calls `set_wake({mode: "standby", standby_agent: "<name>"})` with that bot. Without one, it calls `set_wake({mode: "none"})`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    In the chat it calls `send_message` with ✅ installed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How your AI listens

- Grok on the web cannot be woken from outside. When you write first, zapbip hands your message to your Grok Bot, which answers and keeps the conversation going.
- Without a Grok Bot, your message waits until you open the Grok chat, and zapbip tells you on WhatsApp that Grok is not listening right now.

There is no time limit. While a question is open, your AI asks again until you answer, for as long as it takes.
