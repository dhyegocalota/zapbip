Pick the AI you use and open its page. Each page shows the same four things: what works, how that AI hears you, how to set it up from zero, and how to check it works. All of them send you messages, ask with buttons and lists, and wait for your answer for as long as it takes.

The wake type says how your message reaches the AI when you write first from WhatsApp. The expected delay is how long that takes once you write.

## What each wake type means

- **Local:** a connector or listener on your computer wakes the AI the moment you write.
- **Webhook:** zapbip calls a link that belongs to the AI, and the AI starts.
- **Scheduled:** the AI checks for your message on a timer, so it hears you at the next check.
- **Standby:** another AI of yours, a Grok Bot, answers in its place.
- **Inbox:** your message waits until the AI looks for it.

If the AI is not listening right now, your message waits and zapbip tells you so. Nothing is lost.
