- **ChatGPT**: waits for your answer for up to 5 minutes.
- **Claude (app and site)**: waits for your answer for up to 4 minutes.
- **Claude Code**: waits for your answer for as long as needed.
- **Codex**: waits for your answer for 5 minutes, up to 15.
- **Grok**: waits for your answer for up to 1 minute.
- **Grok Bot**: waits for your answer for 2 minutes or more.
- **Any app that supports MCP**: depends on the app.

The zapbip number is coming soon, so every AI is listed as coming soon. This list shows what each one will do. You cannot start the conversation from any of them yet.

## Starting the conversation

Today, if you write first and your AI is not listening, your message waits and zapbip tells you the AI is not listening right now. Waking each AI so it answers right away is coming soon.
