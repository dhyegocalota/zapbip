title: Supported agents
description: Which AI you can connect, and what each one can do.
---

- **ChatGPT**: waits for your answer for up to 5 minutes.
- **Claude (app and site)**: waits for your answer for up to 4 minutes.
- **Claude Code**: waits for your answer for as long as needed.
- **Codex**: waits for your answer for 5 minutes, up to 15.
- **Grok**: waits for your answer for up to 1 minute.
- **Grok Bot**: waits for your answer for 2 minutes or more.
- **Any app that supports MCP**: depends on the app.

You can start the conversation with any of them from WhatsApp, through the zapbip number.

## Starting the conversation

Each AI keeps listening with no time limit, in the way its app allows: a channel or a background listener for Claude Code, a local connector for Codex, a webhook for Grok Bot, a scheduled task for claude.ai and ChatGPT, a standby for Grok on the web, and a plugin for Hermes and OpenClaw. Write first and your message reaches the AI; if it is not listening right now, your message waits and zapbip tells you so.
