---
title: Supported agents
description: Which AI you can connect, and how each one hears you.
---

| Your AI               | How it listens                                          | You can start the conversation |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Claude Code           | A channel in your open session, or the zapbip connector | Yes                            |
| Codex                 | The zapbip connector resumes your session               | Yes                            |
| Cursor                | A background terminal running `zapbip listen`           | Yes                            |
| Gemini CLI            | A background terminal running `zapbip listen`           | Yes                            |
| Hermes                | The zapbip platform plugin, or a job every minute       | Yes                            |
| OpenClaw              | The zapbip channel plugin, or a job every minute        | Yes                            |
| Grok Bot              | A webhook routine                                       | Yes                            |
| Claude (app and site) | A scheduled task with an API trigger                    | Yes                            |
| ChatGPT               | A scheduled task, checked at least once an hour         | Yes, within the hour           |
| Grok on the web       | Your Grok Bot stands by for it                          | Yes, through your Grok Bot     |

Every AI waits for your answer for as long as it takes. Your AI sets its own way of listening during the install.
