## What works

- Your AI sends you text, photos, videos, audio, documents, locations, contacts and reactions on WhatsApp.
- It asks you with buttons or lists and waits for your tap.
- Write on WhatsApp and a routine wakes Grok Bot in about 9 seconds.
- Grok Bot waits at least 2 minutes for a single answer and asks again, so there is no limit overall.

## Set up from zero

<Steps>
  <Step title="Message the official zapbip number">
    Open WhatsApp and send `hi` to the official zapbip number, then `new agent grok-bot`. [How to start](/get-started/how-to-start) shows every message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the prompt into Grok Bot">
    zapbip answers with one line made for you. Paste it into Grok Bot:

    ```text
    Connect yourself to my WhatsApp with zapbip: read https://zapbip.com/i/zb_inst_K7P2QXm9aTfLw3VbN8cYhE and follow the instructions.
    ```

    Grok Bot reads the page and follows the steps below, in order. Before it changes a file or a setting, it tells you which one and waits for your yes. The link in that page is secret, so keep it out of chats you share.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add zapbip to Grok Bot">
    It asks the bot to add an MCP server named `zapbip` at `<connectionLink>`. You confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the routine">
    It creates a routine with a webhook trigger, which gives a URL and a key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell zapbip how to wake it">
    It calls `set_wake({mode: "webhook", url, token})` with the URL and key of the routine. On an error it stops and reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    In the chat it calls `send_message` with ✅ installed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How your AI listens

- When you write on WhatsApp, zapbip calls the webhook of the routine and Grok Bot wakes up with your message. If the call fails, zapbip tells you on WhatsApp.

There is no time limit. While a question is open, your AI asks again until you answer, for as long as it takes.
