## What works

- Your AI sends you text, photos, videos, audio, documents, locations, contacts and reactions on WhatsApp.
- It asks you with buttons or lists and waits for your tap.
- Write on WhatsApp and Hermes hears you within about a minute.
- It waits for your answer as long as it takes.

## Set up from zero

<Steps>
  <Step title="Message the official zapbip number">
    Open WhatsApp and send `hi` to the official zapbip number, then `new agent hermes`. [How to start](/get-started/how-to-start) shows every message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the prompt into Hermes">
    zapbip answers with one line made for you. Paste it into Hermes:

    ```text
    Connect yourself to my WhatsApp with zapbip: read https://zapbip.com/i/K7P2QX and follow the instructions.
    ```

    Hermes reads the page and follows the steps below, in order. Before it changes a file or a setting, it tells you which one and waits for your yes. The link in that page is secret, so keep it out of chats you share.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add zapbip to Hermes">
    It adds an MCP server named `zapbip` at `<connectionLink>`, with its `url` and `headers`, under `mcp_servers` in `config.yaml`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the platform plugin">
    The better option: it installs and enables the zapbip platform plugin with `ZAPBIP_ALLOWED_USERS=owner` set, because Hermes otherwise refuses your messages, then calls `set_wake({mode: "local"})`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or keep a background watcher">
    Only if the plugin cannot be installed, and never together with it, it creates a `cronjob_manage` job every minute that calls `check_inbox`. It then calls `set_wake({mode: "scheduled", interval_minutes: 1})`. A watcher that polls costs model calls, and Hermes tells you so.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    In the chat it calls `send_message` with ✅ installed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How your AI listens

- With the zapbip platform plugin, each WhatsApp message arrives in Hermes as it is sent.
- Without it, Hermes runs a job every minute that checks the inbox. The plugin and the job are never used together.

There is no time limit. While a question is open, your AI asks again until you answer, for as long as it takes.
