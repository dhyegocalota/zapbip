---
title: 'Account deletion'
description: 'How the owner deletes the account and what that removes.'
---

The owner can delete the account at any time, from WhatsApp. Deletion takes two messages, so a stray message cannot trigger it.

## The two steps

1. Send `apagar conta` (or `delete account`). zapbip replies with the consequences and the exact phrase to confirm.
2. Send the phrase `apagar conta definitivamente` (or `delete account permanently`) within 10 minutes. After that window the phrase is refused and nothing is deleted; send `apagar conta` again to start over.

## What is removed

- The account's inbox, with every message, question and pending reply.
- Every agent connection. Their links stop working at once.
- The media stored for the account.
- Your phone number and the account record.

If a step fails, the account stays in the deleting state. Your next message, or the operator, runs the remaining steps. Each step is safe to repeat.

## What is not removed

- Meta keeps the WhatsApp history on its side. zapbip cannot delete it. Delete the chat in WhatsApp if you want it off your phone.
- Cloudflare keeps point-in-time copies of the database and the inbox for its own retention period.
- If the operator blocked your number, the block stays.

## A new account after deletion

A message from the same phone after deletion creates a new account, as for any first message. It starts on the waitlist when access is limited, and has no agents and no history.

See [agents and links](/guides/agents-and-links) to revoke a single agent without deleting the account.
