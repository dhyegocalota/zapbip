---
title: The 24-hour window
description: Why a message may wait, and what you get on your phone.
---

WhatsApp lets zapbip write freely only within 24 hours of your last message. zapbip closes its window 10 minutes early.

## Open

Messages arrive at once. The tool answers `delivery: "sent"`.

## Closed

The tool answers `delivery: "queued"`. You receive one notice that agents are waiting, and the messages arrive in order as soon as you write anything. Queued messages older than 7 days expire.

## Ask your agent

```text
Send me "nightly job finished".
```

If you have not written for a day, you see one notice instead. Reply with anything and the message follows.

## Try it locally

Move the simulator clock forward a day:

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:8787/dev/owner/5511999990000/clock \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"advance_ms":86400000}'
```
