---
title: The 24-hour window
description: Why a message may wait, and what you get on your phone.
---

WhatsApp lets zapbip write to you freely only within 24 hours of your last message to it.

## Window open

Messages from your AI arrive at once.

## Window closed

You get one notice saying your AIs have messages waiting. Write anything and they arrive, in order. Messages older than 7 days expire.

## What you type into your AI

```text
Send me "nightly job finished".
```

If you have not written for a day, you see the notice first. Reply with anything and the message follows.
