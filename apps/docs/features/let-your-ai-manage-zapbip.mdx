Everything you do in the [web app](/features/web-app) or with a [WhatsApp command](/features/whatsapp-commands), your AI can do for you. You stay in charge: a change needs your tap on WhatsApp, and nothing happens without it.

## What your AI can do

| Ask your AI to | What happens | Needs your tap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| List your AIs | It reads each name, the app it connected from, when it was created and when it was last active. | No |
| Read your account | It reads the state of your account, your phone number with the middle hidden and your plan. | No |
| Connect a new AI | zapbip asks you on WhatsApp, then connects it. | Yes |
| Disconnect an AI | zapbip asks you on WhatsApp, then stops that AI at once. | Yes |
| Open the web app | zapbip sends you the sign-in link on WhatsApp. | No |

Deleting your account is not on the list. It stays a WhatsApp command that only you can write.

## How the tap works

When your AI asks to connect a new AI named `helper`, zapbip writes to you on WhatsApp, as the AI that asked:

- **Allow** connects it and sends the link to paste into that AI to you, as `new agent` does.
- **Allow, send to agent** connects it and hands the link to the AI that asked, so it can finish the setup itself.
- **Deny** changes nothing.

Disconnecting has two buttons, Allow and Deny. Each question ends with a short reference, so an answer to an old question is never used for a new one.

Your AI waits up to 50 seconds for your answer. If you do not answer in time, nothing changes, and a tap after that changes nothing either. Ask your AI again if you still want it.

## What stays private

- The link to connect a new AI goes to you on WhatsApp. Only the button **Allow, send to agent** gives it to your AI.
- The sign-in link goes to you only. Your AI never sees it.
- Your AI never sees a key, a code or your full phone number.

## Limits

Each connection can start 3 changes a minute. The 4th gets a short "try again shortly" answer, so a mistaken loop cannot fill your WhatsApp with questions.
