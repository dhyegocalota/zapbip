## Ask your agent

```text
Before deploying, ask me with the options Yes and No, and wait for my answer.
```

## What you see

```text
claude-code     Can I deploy v2?
                [ Yes ]  [ No ]
```

Up to 3 options show as buttons, 4 to 10 as a list. A tap, or a typed reply, answers the question.

## If you are slow

The agent waits up to 50 seconds per call and gets `pending` with a `question_id`. It keeps waiting with [wait_for_reply](/mcp-reference/wait-for-reply), and your answer is never lost. Details in [Questions](/concepts/questions).
