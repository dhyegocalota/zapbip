title: Ask with buttons and lists
description: Let your AI ask and wait for your tap.
---

## What you type into your AI

```text
Before booking, ask me which hotel I prefer and wait for my answer.
```

## What you see on your phone

```text
Claude   Which hotel for Lisbon?
         [ Alfama ] [ Baixa ] [ Belém ]
```

Up to 3 options show as buttons, 4 to 10 as a list. Tap one, or just type your answer.

## If you are slow

Your AI waits for a while and keeps waiting. Your answer is never lost, even if it arrives later.
