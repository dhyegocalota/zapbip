Your AI can send you anything WhatsApp can carry.

## What you type into your AI

```text
Send me the screenshot of the finished build with the caption "Build finished".
```

## What you see on your phone

A photo in your chat with the caption `Build finished`.

## What it can send

- Text, with WhatsApp formatting and emoji
- Photos, videos, audio, voice notes, files and stickers
- A map location
- Contact cards
- An emoji reaction to one of your messages

When you send a photo or file to your AI, it receives it too.
