You manage your AIs by writing to zapbip. A command is a short phrase in English or Portuguese, and zapbip answers in your language. Each row shows the English phrase with the Portuguese one beside it.

| You want to         | Write in English                                 | Or in Portuguese                                      | What happens                                   |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Connect a new AI    | `new agent claude`                               | `novo agente claude`                                  | zapbip sends the line to paste into that AI    |
| See your AIs        | `agents`                                         | `agentes`                                             | A list with the name of each one               |
| Disconnect an AI    | `revoke claude`                                  | `revogar claude`                                      | That AI stops working at once                  |
| Get a new link      | `rotate claude`                                  | `girar claude`                                        | The old link stops and you get a fresh one     |
| Open the menu       | `help` or `menu`                                 | `ajuda` or `menu`                                     | The commands as text and as a tappable list    |
| Delete your account | `delete account`, then `delete account permanently` | `apagar conta`, then `apagar conta definitivamente` | See [delete your account](/help/delete-your-account) |

Write the phrase alone: a message that is exactly a command word is a command. To send the same words to an AI, start with its name: `@claude help` reaches Claude instead of opening the menu.

## Names

A name has 1 to 20 lowercase letters, numbers or hyphens. The words that start a command are reserved and cannot name an AI: `new`, `agents`, `revoke`, `rotate`, `help`, `menu`, `delete` and their Portuguese versions.

## Forwarded messages

A forwarded message is never a command, even when its text is a command word. It goes to your AI like any other message.
