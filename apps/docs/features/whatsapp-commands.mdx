You manage your AIs by writing to the zapbip number. A command is a short phrase, in Portuguese or English. The language you write is the language of the answer. Each row shows both versions.

| You want to            | Write in English                                    | Or in Portuguese                                    | What happens                                                                |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Connect a new AI       | `new agent claude`                                  | `novo agente claude`                                | zapbip answers with the prompt to paste into that AI                        |
| See your AIs           | `agents`                                            | `agentes`                                           | A list with the name and the date of each one                               |
| Disconnect an AI       | `revoke claude`                                     | `revogar claude`                                    | That AI stops working at once                                               |
| Get a new prompt       | `rotate claude`                                     | `girar claude`                                      | The old connection stops and you get a fresh prompt                         |
| Sign in to the web app | `login`                                             | `entrar`                                            | A link that works once for 10 minutes, see [the web app](/features/web-app) |
| Open the menu          | `help` or `menu`                                    | `ajuda` or `menu`                                   | The commands as text and as a tappable list                                 |
| Delete your account    | `delete account`, then `delete account permanently` | `apagar conta`, then `apagar conta definitivamente` | See [delete your account](/help/delete-your-account)                        |

Write the phrase alone. A trailing `?` or `!` makes it a normal message for your AIs.

## Talking to an AI with a command word

A message that is exactly a command word is always a command. To send the same words to an AI, start with its name: `@claude help` reaches Claude instead of opening the menu.

## Names

The words `new`, `agents`, `revoke`, `rotate`, `help`, `menu`, `login`, `delete` and their Portuguese versions are reserved: they cannot be the name of an AI.

## Forwarded messages

A forwarded message is never a command, even when its text is a command word. It goes to your AIs like any other message.
