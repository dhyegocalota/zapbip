title: The web app
description: Sign in to a small web page with your WhatsApp, see your AIs, connect one and disconnect another.
---

Everything you can do on WhatsApp you can still do on WhatsApp. The web app is a small page for the two things that are easier to see than to type: the list of your AIs and a button to connect a new one.

## Sign in

1. Open [app.zapbip.com](https://app.zapbip.com) and tap **Sign in with WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with the word `login` ready to send. You can also write `login` to the zapbip number yourself.
2. zapbip answers with a link. It works once and expires in 10 minutes.
3. Open the link and tap **Continue**.

There is no password and no code to type: your WhatsApp number is your identity. Do not share the link; whoever opens it first signs in as you.

If the link expired, write `login` again. A new link replaces the old one.

## What you see

- **While you are on the waitlist**, a short note that your access is not open yet, and **Sign out**.
- **With access**, your AIs with the app each one connected from and the date it was created, a **Revoke** button for each, a form to **Connect an agent**, the plan line and **Sign out**.

**Connect an agent** works like `new agent` on WhatsApp: the page shows the prompt to paste into that AI. It is shown once. **Revoke** asks you to confirm and then stops that AI at once, like `revoke` on WhatsApp.

Deleting your account stays on WhatsApp. The page links to [delete your account](/help/delete-your-account).

## Your AI and the web app

An AI can use the page like you do, and it can read it more reliably: the home page answers `Accept: application/json` with the same data it shows, your AIs, the state of your account and your plan, or `401` while no one is signed in. Buttons stay buttons, so an AI in a browser submits them like you. An AI connected to zapbip does not need the browser for any of this: see [let your AI manage zapbip](/features/let-your-ai-manage-zapbip).

## Sign out

**Sign out** ends the session on that device. A session also ends when you delete your account, and after 30 days.

## Language

The page follows the language of your account after you sign in, and has an English and a Portuguese link at the top.
