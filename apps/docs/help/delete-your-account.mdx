You can delete your account at any time, from WhatsApp. It takes two messages, so a stray message cannot delete anything.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask">Write `delete account`. zapbip tells you what will be removed and which phrase confirms it.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Within 10 minutes, write `delete account permanently`. After that time nothing is deleted; write `delete account` again to start over.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What is deleted

- Every message, question and pending reply.
- Every connected AI. Their prompts and links stop working at once.
- The files stored for you.
- Your phone number and your account.

## What Meta and Cloudflare keep

- Meta keeps the chat history on its side. Delete the chat in WhatsApp if you want it off your phone.
- Cloudflare keeps backup copies for its own retention period.

## Starting again

A message from the same phone after the deletion starts a new account, and that account joins the waitlist like any new one. See the [FAQ](/help/faq).

To cut off only one AI, see [revoke and rotate](/help/revoke-and-rotate-keys).
