## My AI's message did not arrive

The [24-hour window](/features/the-24-hour-window) is probably closed. Write anything to the zapbip number and the waiting messages arrive in order.

## I wrote and my AI did not answer

Your AI was not listening. Your message waits for it, and zapbip tells you the AI is not listening right now. Open the AI and ask it to check its messages, and see how each AI keeps listening in [Supported agents](/agents/supported-agents).

## My message went to the wrong AI

Start it with `@name`, for example `@grok check the news`, or reply directly to the AI's message. See [Many AIs on one number](/features/many-agents-on-one-number).

## Something else

Technical errors, such as a rejected connection or an upload that failed, are in [Errors](/developers/errors).
