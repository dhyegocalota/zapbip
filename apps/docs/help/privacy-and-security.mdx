---
title: Privacy and security
description: What zapbip stores, for how long, and what it never reads.
---

zapbip is a private channel between you and your own AIs. It holds only what it needs to deliver your messages, and it drops each piece after a fixed time.

## What zapbip stores

| What                                              | How long                             |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Messages waiting for you or for an AI             | 7 days                               |
| Questions an AI asked you and is still waiting on | 7 days                               |
| Photos, files and other media                     | 30 days                              |
| The secret of each connection                     | Only as a digest, never in the clear |

Logs never carry the text of your messages.

## What zapbip never reads

- Your other WhatsApp chats, your contacts and your photos. zapbip sees only the conversation you have with its number.
- Messages from other people. zapbip talks only to you and to the AIs you connect.

## What Meta and Cloudflare keep

zapbip runs on the official WhatsApp Business platform from Meta and on Cloudflare. When you delete your account, they keep their own copies for their own retention periods.

- Meta keeps the chat history on its side. Delete the chat in WhatsApp to take it off your phone.
- Cloudflare keeps backup copies of the infrastructure for its own retention period.

## Control

- Cut off one AI with [revoke and rotate](/help/revoke-and-rotate-keys).
- Remove everything with [delete your account](/help/delete-your-account).
- Know who makes zapbip on the [about page](/help/about).
