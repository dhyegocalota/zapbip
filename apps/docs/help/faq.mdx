---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use it today with my phone?">
    Yes. Message the zapbip number on WhatsApp and see [How to start](/get-started/how-to-start).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my own WhatsApp number?">
    Not yet. Every AI texts you from the zapbip number, and you use your normal WhatsApp, so there is nothing to register.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which AIs work?">See [Supported agents](/agents/supported-agents).</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it talk to other people?">No. zapbip talks only to you, through the official WhatsApp Cloud API.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can several AIs share one number?">Yes. See [Many AIs on one number](/features/many-agents-on-one-number).</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my message not arrive at once?">
    The 24-hour window is closed. See [The 24-hour window](/features/the-24-hour-window).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to be a developer?">No. zapbip is for anyone who uses AI. The developer pages are optional.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my account is on the waitlist?">
    The account is held until the zapbip team activates it. You wait; there is nothing else to do. When access opens, send `help` to see the
    commands.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I stop an AI?">Write `revoke` and its name. See [revoke and rotate](/help/revoke-and-rotate-keys).</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I delete everything?">
    Write `delete account` on WhatsApp. See [delete your account](/help/delete-your-account).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does zapbip keep?">See [privacy and security](/help/privacy-and-security).</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
