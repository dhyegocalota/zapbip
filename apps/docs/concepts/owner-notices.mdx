Outside the [24-hour window](/concepts/24-hour-window) WhatsApp only delivers approved templates. zapbip registers two, in the owner's language (Portuguese or English).

| Template                | Sent when                 | Body (English)                                               | Quick reply |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------- |
| `zapbip_agent_notice`   | an agent sent a message   | `Message from {{1}}: {{2}} — tap Open to read it.`           | `Open`      |
| `zapbip_agent_question` | an agent asked a question | `Question from {{1}}: {{2}} — tap Reply to see the options.` | `Reply`     |

`{{1}}` is the agent name and `{{2}}` is a preview of the content, at most 60 characters on one line. A preview of a non-text message is its kind in brackets, such as `[image]`. The Portuguese bodies and the quick replies `Abrir` and `Responder` are registered under the same names.

## Why the notice comes first

A notice carries none of the real content. The message, or the question with its buttons or list, follows it, because a free-form message is only allowed once the owner has written.

The owner taps the quick reply. That tap is a message from the owner: it opens the window and the queue is delivered in order. The tap is the only action a notice offers and it is never forwarded to an agent.

zapbip sends one notice per closed window. A notice that zapbip writes itself, such as the one for an agent that is not listening, follows the same rule.

## When the template is rejected

If WhatsApp rejects the template, the message stays queued and the send that asked for it returns `delivery: "failed"` with a message saying the notice failed. zapbip tries the notice again after 5 minutes, then after 10, 20 and so on, doubling up to 6 hours. A notice zapbip wrote itself has no agent to tell, so a rejection is only logged. The queued messages are delivered when the owner next writes.
