Outside the [24-hour window](/concepts/24-hour-window) WhatsApp only delivers approved templates. zapbip uses two, in the owner's language (Portuguese or English).

| Template | When | Quick reply |
|---|---|---|
| `zapbip_agent_notice` | an agent sent a message | `Abrir` / `Open` |
| `zapbip_agent_question` | an agent asked a question | `Responder` / `Reply` |

Each names the agent and shows a preview of at most 60 characters on one line.

## Why the notice comes first

A notice arrives before the real content and carries none of its own. The real message, or the question with its buttons or list, follows it, because a free-form message is only allowed once the owner has written.

The owner taps the quick reply. That tap is a message from the owner: it opens the window and the queue is delivered in order.

zapbip sends one notice per closed window. The tap is the only action a notice offers, and it is never forwarded to an agent.
