import { Chat } from '/snippets/chat.jsx';
import OfficialNumber from '/snippets/official-number.mdx';
import InstallPrompt from '/snippets/install-prompt.mdx';

1. **Message the zapbip number on WhatsApp.** Send any text, for example `hi`. Your first message creates your account and proves the phone is yours.

   <OfficialNumber />

   <Chat
     messages={[
       { you: true, text: 'hi', time: '09:00' },
       { name: 'zapbip', text: 'No agent is connected. Your message is saved.', time: '09:00' },
     ]}
   />

2. **Ask for a new agent.** An agent is one AI you connect. Send `new agent` and a name, for example `new agent claude`.

3. **Get your prompt.** zapbip answers with one or two lines made for you.

   <Chat
     messages={[
       { you: true, text: 'new agent claude', time: '09:01' },
       {
         name: 'zapbip',
         text: 'Agent claude created. Paste this into your agent: Connect yourself to my WhatsApp with zapbip: read https://mcp.zapbip.com/i/zb_inst_<code> and follow the instructions. This link works once for 30 minutes. Do not share it. If the link expires or was used, send "rotate claude".',
         time: '09:01',
       },
     ]}
   />

4. **Paste it into your AI.** Open ChatGPT, Claude, Grok or the AI you use, and paste the line zapbip sent you. It looks like this:

   <InstallPrompt />

   Your AI reads the instructions online and sets itself up.

5. **Your AI messages you.** It sends a first message on WhatsApp to confirm it is connected.

   <Chat messages={[{ name: 'Claude', text: '✅ installed', time: '09:03' }]} />

<Note>
  claude.ai, ChatGPT and Grok on the web ask one thing of you: your AI will tell you to add a connector in its settings. Follow what it
  says; it is a rule of those apps.
</Note>

## What to expect

- One WhatsApp number for all your AIs, and a line for each one so you can tell them apart.
- The line works once and expires after 30 minutes. If it expires, send `rotate claude` for a new one.
- You can write first at any time, and your AI keeps listening with no time limit.
- On the waitlist? See [the FAQ](/help/faq) for what happens next.
- Pick your AI and follow its page in [Supported agents](/agents/supported-agents).
