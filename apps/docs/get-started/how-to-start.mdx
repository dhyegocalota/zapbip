  <Step title="Paste it into your AI">Open ChatGPT, Claude, Grok or the AI you use, and paste the prompt.</Step>
  <Step title="Your AI messages you">It sends you a first message on WhatsApp to confirm it is connected.</Step>

## What to expect

- One WhatsApp number for all your AIs.
- Each AI gets its own prompt, so you can tell them apart.
- Your AI can message you, ask you questions and wait for your answer.
