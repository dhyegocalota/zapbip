---
title: How to start
description: From your WhatsApp to your first message from your AI.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Say hi to the zapbip number">
    Open WhatsApp and send `hi` to the zapbip number. That is how zapbip knows the phone is yours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a new agent">
    Send `new agent` and a name, for example `new agent claude`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your one-line prompt">
    zapbip answers with a single line made for you:

    ```text
    Connect yourself to my WhatsApp with zapbip: read https://zapbip.com/i/K7P2QX and follow the instructions.
    ```

  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into your AI">
    Open ChatGPT, Claude, Grok or the AI you use, and paste the line. Your AI reads the instructions and sets itself up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your AI messages you">
    It sends you a first message on WhatsApp to confirm it is connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  claude.ai, ChatGPT and Grok on the web require one thing from you: your AI will ask you to paste a link into its settings. Follow what it
  tells you; it is a rule of those apps.
</Note>

## What to expect

- One WhatsApp number for all your AIs.
- Each AI gets its own line, so you can tell them apart.
- The line works once and expires after a short time.
